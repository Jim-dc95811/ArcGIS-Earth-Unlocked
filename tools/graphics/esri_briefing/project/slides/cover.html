ArcGIS Earth Unlocked

Seven free projects that add five missing capabilities to ArcGIS Earth, built and field-tested by a fire-service radio engineer.

Jim G · github.com/Jim-dc95811 · November 2026

Open with the one-line story: ArcGIS Earth is already a great 3D globe and renderer. These seven free projects fill five gaps around it: making offline maps, flying it, travelling city to city, placing objects, and turning giant pictures into worlds. Everything shown runs today on ArcGIS Earth Desktop 3.0.1.
