ArcGIS Earth Unlocked

9

PointLayer pulls a whole public ArcGIS point layer, with completeness checks, and writes an offline KMZ with level-of-detail settings: 3,250 features in Nassau County, 30,688 in Duval. Parcel100 fills the same 100-cell Master 4 grid with clickable parcel GeoJSON from Florida’s statewide parcel service. The PRAVE/ME Tracker reads radio GPS reports from a serial port and draws each unit through the Automation API, with signal strength shown on the icon.
