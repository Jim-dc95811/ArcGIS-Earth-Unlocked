Built for the Thrustmaster TCA Sidestick Airbus Edition. The stick reads through Windows (WinMM) and every move becomes a camera PUT to ArcGIS Earth’s local Automation API. Recommended style is two hands: stick in one, mouse in the other for clicking popups. The readout shows eye altitude from sea level, compass heading and an equivalent web zoom level.
