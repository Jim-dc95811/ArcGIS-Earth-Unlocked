ArcGIS Earth Unlocked

2

ArcGIS Earth already renders TPKX offline, flies smoothly and has a powerful local Automation API. What it lacks are the pieces around it: a simple way to make offline maps, a flight controller, a way to hop between places, quick object authoring, and a way to turn any big picture into a place you can fly into. The SpaceMouse support is real and documented by Esri; a flight stick is not supported natively.
