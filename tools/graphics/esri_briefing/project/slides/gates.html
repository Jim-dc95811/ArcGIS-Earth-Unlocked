Gates are ordinary KMZ files: the visible square gate is KML geometry, and EarthStick reads the gate data from the same file. Fly through the gold side and the camera jumps to the partner gate. City boards carry clickable stars whose popups draw coloured beacons through the Automation API. World Gate Maker turns two places into a gate pair; Jim flew Jacksonville to Seattle and back with one.
