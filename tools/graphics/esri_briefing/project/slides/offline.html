mb2tpkx copies 256-pixel PNG or JPEG tiles from a QGIS MBTiles file straight into an Esri Compact Cache V2 TPKX, unchanged. Field-proven at 4 GB, 20+ GB and 40 GB-class packages. Master 4 gives every 0.1-degree cell a number and a file name pattern, so statewide map sets stay organised. On Android about 2 GB at Z17 is the practical ceiling. Batch Factory is an ArcGIS Pro 3.7 add-in for people who do have Pro. The tools make maps; the imagery source and its terms are the user's choice.
