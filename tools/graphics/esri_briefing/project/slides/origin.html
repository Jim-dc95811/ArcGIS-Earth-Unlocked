Where it came from

First responders needed sharp satellite maps with no signal. The tracking system they used stopped at zoom 17.

Built in the field

37 years of electronics and radio work at a state fire department, applied to mapping.

Built with AI

Conceived, directed and tested by a non-programmer, written with ChatGPT and Claude.

Given away

Free and public on GitHub, written for people with no GIS training.

ArcGIS Earth Unlocked

3

The work started with offline imagery for first responders: Z20 imagery on a laptop or phone with no connection. Learning how tile pyramids and zoom levels work led to the TPKX focus, and ArcGIS Earth became the common renderer for everything else. The mission line: do not make the user learn GIS before they can use geography.
