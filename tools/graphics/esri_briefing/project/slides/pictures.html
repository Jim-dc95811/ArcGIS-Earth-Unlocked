Picture World Maker places a flat picture on the globe at a fixed spot, one metre per pixel, and tiles it straight into a TPKX that ArcGIS Earth opens like any map. The test picture was a 50,000 by 12,006 pixel London panorama: 35,216 tiles, zoom 0 to 18, built in 55 seconds on a Windows PC, about 442 MB. The three views are 61,246 ft, 4,203 ft and 786 ft in the same picture. Pictures with map coordinates (GeoTIFF) ArcGIS Earth already opens directly. Twenty World spots park each picture in its own one-degree box of open Atlantic, so a gallery sits side by side - the Babel to the Shard Short flies between two of them.
